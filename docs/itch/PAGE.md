## Description (anglais en premier, français dessous)

**Start alone in your bedroom. End as an AI empire.**

You run a one-person automation agency. Every click runs a task. Every euro hires an AI agent: a workflow bot, a copywriter, an analyst, an auditor, a legal agent… Each one sits at its desk on your porcelain office floor, as a swarm of ink letters writing its work in real time.

- 7 kinds of AI agents, each with its own desk and its own swarm of letters
- 6 clients to sign, from the corner bistro to government contracts
- Short contracts and optional incidents to keep you sharp
- Prestige to start over stronger, plus permanent reputation perks
- Your agents keep working while you're away
- Plays in English or French, on desktop and phone, with no account or download
